Source and geographic caveats

Statistics: Statistisches Landesamt Bremen, supplied Datentabelle.xls; shared ortsteil_analysis_ready.csv. Exact source worksheets and labels appear above.

Boundaries: Landesamt GeoInformation Bremen, ATKIS Verwaltungsgrenzen, CC BY 4.0. Dataset revision: 11 December 2024; retrieved 10 October 2026. Original EPSG:25832 polygons; displayed with an affine SVG transform, no tiles or fabricated coordinates.

87 official Ortsteil polygons plus the documented undivided Oberneuland Stadtteil crosswalk to statistical 361. All 88 represented mainland records match; the separate overseas-port enclave and Bremerhaven are outside this input scope. Exact legal boundary-validity and statistical reference days remain unverified. Basic geometry checks do not certify topology.

Descriptive ecological evidence, not causal, individual-level or population-weighted city statistics. Missingness reasons are unknown. This prototype is not a deployed municipal service.
