# Sources and verification limits

Review date: 2026-10-09. Statistical observations come exclusively from the supplied local sources. This is technical evidence, not a literature review or a final report section.

| Source | Use | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Datentabelle.xls`, supplied Bremer Ortsteilatlas export | Primary annual Ortsteil percentages and historical observations | All worksheets reread, reconstructed values compared to Phase 1 outputs. File does not establish download date, version, exact reference dates or full definitions. |
| 22 `ST*.csv` sources | Historical provenance and preparation audit | All reread using CP1252, semicolon and decimal comma. Kept separate from scoring. |
| `bremen_data.json` | Open-data acquisition catalogue | All metadata records read separately; catalogue timestamps/titles are not observation years. |
| [Official Bremer Ortsteilatlas page](https://www.statistik.bremen.de/datenangebote/bremer-ortsteilatlas-15228) | Source identity | Search located the official atlas page; opening returned HTTP 403. Detailed current online indicator definitions remain unverified. |
| [Bremen statistical yearbook 2011](https://www.statistik.bremen.de/sixcms/media.php/13/jb2011.pdf) | Historical geographic warning | Official search excerpt identifies conversion of Handelshaefen to Ortsteil 437 Ueberseestadt in 2009. This is evidence of a change, not a complete boundary crosswalk. |
| [Die Ortsteile der Stadt Bremen 2015](https://www.statistik.bremen.de/sixcms/media.php/13/Ortsteilheft2015.pdf) | Historical reference-date context | Official PDF opened. Its contents distinguish June employment/unemployment and December benefit data. These historical dates cannot be assumed to establish the 2024 dates. |
| [BA: SGB II receipt and unemployment](https://statistik.arbeitsagentur.de/DE/Statischer-Content/Service/Mediathek/Generische-Publikationen/Textbox-ELB-Alo.html?nn=24836) | Conceptual distinction | Official source explains that benefit entitlement and unemployment are distinct statuses. It does not verify the supplied atlas denominator or revision history. |
| [BA: introduction of Buergergeld](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/rd-nsb/ueber-uns/buergergeld) | Time-series warning | Official source states Buergergeld replaced the earlier benefits in January 2023. A label continuing to say SGB II is not proof of unchanged eligibility or comparability. |

Before publishing longitudinal claims, obtain municipal reference-date and revision notes, historical geometry/crosswalks, and SGB II definition-break documentation. For present annual scores, use wording such as "relative disadvantage on three selected 2024 indicators among 79 covered Ortsteile." Scores are not official Bremen classifications.

No external academic findings, international benchmark values, geometry or individual data were imported. Source URLs are supporting verification references, not fabricated academic citations.
