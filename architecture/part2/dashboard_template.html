From evidence to a decision

A proposed human review sequence for evaluating options.

  1. Inspect indicators. Check coverage, definitions, and saved results.
  2. Review local context. Consider coverage gaps, local knowledge, and any future approved observations.
  3. Investigate service needs. Use relevant service data and feedback to examine specific needs.
  4. Compare interventions. Record the alternatives and the evidence behind each.
  5. Assess cost and feasibility. Establish resources, constraints, and practical delivery conditions.
  6. Make a human-reviewed decision. Document rationale and any pilot baseline, outcome measure, and review date.
  7. Monitor outcomes. Review evidence and decide whether to adapt, stop, or extend any approved pilot.

Methodological limitations

Scores use three selected indicators for complete cases. Rankings depend on method and coverage. Exact within-year source dates and denominator counts remain unverified. Missing inputs are not imputed. Results describe areas in a single reporting year and support no individual-level, longitudinal, or causal conclusions.
